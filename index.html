PalBoard

Live

A Palworld save dashboard that runs entirely in your browser.

palboard.app/#/

The whole world on one screen. The status bar reports the parse itself: codec, ratio, timing and warnings.

Drop your save folder on the page and PalBoard decodes a 32 MB proprietary binary format the game never documented. No upload, no account, no install, and no write path anywhere near your world.

  • ~0.5 sto open a 32 MB Unreal Engine save, Oodle-compressed, through a WebAssembly decoder running in a Web Worker.
  • 0 bytesuploaded. The page's Content-Security-Policy lists no remote origin, so an accidental upload would fail before leaving the browser.
  • 6.7M → 1.7Mgold. Summing every container on the island overcounts; resolving which chests stand in your bases gives the real figure.
  • 135 testsacross parser, compression, inventory and watcher, plus a browser driver that fails on any overflow at phone width.

02

Somni

Pre-launch

A new bedtime story every night, read aloud in your own voice.

somni.app/

Forty story elements in four groups, hard caps to keep it focused, and an age dial that retunes everything.

Pick who is in the story and where it happens, set your child's age, and Claude writes an original story tuned to that age. It comes back narrated by a clone of your voice, lighting up each word as it is spoken. Stories and audio never leave the browser.

  • Age 2–7The dial retunes the whole spec: page count, words per page, longest sentence, plot shape and how much tension is allowed.
  • 90 sof audio makes the clone. Echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto-gain are off, because they flattened the delivery the clone learns from.
  • 80 msThe reader runs ahead of the audio clock, since currentTime is the decode position, not the moment sound leaves the speaker.
  • >1.5 sof audio past the last aligned character means invented words, so that segment regenerates before the child ever hears it.

Research

An agent that allocates a portfolio with the downside in mind.

I built a custom Gymnasium market environment and trained a Proximal Policy Optimisation agent to set portfolio weights across stocks, ETFs, commodities, REITs and crypto. It's rewarded for return, penalised for tail risk and charged for every trade, then backtested with monthly rebalancing over 2010 to 2023.

It finished at 10.6× its starting value against the S&P 500's 3.2×, with lower volatility and lower CVaR than the benchmark.

  • 15 assetsin six groups: US tech, SPY and QQQ, gold, oil and silver, three REIT funds, and Bitcoin, Ether and Litecoin.
  • 365 × 125is what the agent sees each step: a year of five FRED macro series plus eight technical indicators per asset.
  • r − ½·CVaRThe reward subtracts half the 5% Conditional Value at Risk, so a strategy that wins most months but blows up in a crash scores badly.
  • turnover²Trading costs grow with the square of turnover to model market impact, which teaches the agent to rebalance gradually instead of churning.

  • Stable-Baselines3
  • Gymnasium
  • 256×256 actor-critic
  • 4 parallel envs
  • FRED · Yahoo · Alpha Vantage
