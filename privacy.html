Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this application ("the App") handles information.

Information We Collect

The App is designed to help users record work hours, breaks, and clock in/out times.

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to the developer.

Data Storage

Any work records you create are stored locally on your device unless you choose to back them up using your own device or cloud services.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use advertising services or collect analytics.

Permissions

The App only requests permissions that are necessary for its functionality. It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or camera unless explicitly stated in future updates.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing.
